# Interface design: walking skeleton (#1)

Builds on the PRD for #1.

One page, at `/`.

## Structure

- The heading `Pantry`.
- The list of items: each shows its name, then its quantity.
- The form: a labelled field `Item`, a labelled field `Quantity`, and a button `Add`.

## States

- Empty: `Nothing in the pantry yet.` in place of the list.
- Refused: the message above the form, with what the cook typed still in the fields.
