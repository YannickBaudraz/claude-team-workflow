# Sources behind /ui-design

For maintainers of `skills/ui-design/`. The skill's own files name no design system, product or author, and carry no system-specific values: a name or a value in an agent's context pulls its work towards that system's look. This file keeps the trail instead, and no skill file points to it.

## REASONING.md

- **Decision planes:** Jesse James Garrett, *The Elements of User Experience* (strategy, scope, structure, skeleton, surface). Alan Cooper, *About Face* (goal-directed design, sovereign and transient postures). Intercom, "Using job stories to design features" (jobs, anxieties, every element traced to a job).
- **Feature before layout:** Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger, *Refactoring UI*.
- **Hierarchy:** NN/g, "Visual hierarchy in UX" (key takeaways first, at most two large elements, three sizes, three contrast levels, the blur test).
- **Tables, dashboards, forms:** NN/g, "Data tables: four major user tasks"; NN/g, "Dashboards: making charts and graphs easier to understand"; Luke Wroblewski, *Web Form Design*.
- **Convention and boldness:** NN/g, "Jakob's law" and "The end of web design".
- **Direction format:** MacLean, Young, Bellotti and Moran, "Questions, options and criteria" (1991); Carroll and Rosson, claims analysis (1992); Jeremy Keith, "Design principles" ("X, even over Y"); Jared Spool on design principles; Intercom, "Running productive design critiques"; Basecamp, *Shape Up* (pitch format).
- **Distinct directions and parallel design:** NN/g, "Parallel and iterative design"; Tohidi, Buxton et al., CHI 2006; Dow et al., "Parallel prototyping", TOCHI 2010; Bill Buxton, *Sketching User Experiences* (elaboration and reduction).
- **Fixation:** Jansson and Smith, "Design fixation", *Design Studies* 1991.

## REVIEWER.md

- **Critique from objectives:** Connor and Irizarry, *Discussing Design*; NN/g, "Design critiques".
- **Severity and evaluators:** Nielsen, severity ratings and heuristic evaluation; Hertzum and Jacobsen, the evaluator effect.
- **LLM critique:** Duan et al., CHI 2024; UICrit, UIST 2024; Panickssery et al., NeurIPS 2024 (self-preference). These tested 2023-2025 models, mostly on mobile screens.
- **Feedback quality:** Krause et al., CHI 2017; CritiqueKit, CHI 2018.
- **Pixel measurement and APCA:** W3C Understanding 1.4.11; APCA documentation and the ARC test notes.

## CRAFT.md

- **Contrast and states:** WCAG 2.2, Understanding 1.4.1, 1.4.3, 1.4.11.
- **Themes:** Material 3 colour roles, colour system and elevation; Material Color Utilities (2021 and 2025 specs); Apple HIG Dark Mode, Color and Accessibility, and WWDC18 session 210; GitHub Primer primitives; Radix Colors; IBM Carbon colour and themes; Microsoft Fluent 2 colour and elevation; Shopify Polaris tokens; Vercel Geist materials; Matthew Ström on colour palettes; Evil Martians on OKLCH; Linear's 2024 UI redesign; APCA documentation and Adrian Roselli on WCAG 3 contrast (April 2026); Piepenbrock et al. 2013 and 2014 on polarity and legibility.
- **Type, space, hierarchy:** Atlassian typography and elevation; Butterick, *Practical Typography*; GOV.UK Design System (tables, error messages, validation); IBM Carbon spacing; NN/g on UI copy.
- **Width:** Material Design responsive layout guidance.
- **States, forms, tables:** NN/g on skeleton screens and data tables; IBM Carbon read-only states and data tables; GOV.UK error messages; Luke Wroblewski on inline validation.

The full research, with every value checked against its source, is in the reports *Designer reasoning directions and craft* and *Light and dark theme rules*.
