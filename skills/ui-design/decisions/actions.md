# Actions, editing surfaces and disclosure

Read this before placing actions and deciding editing surfaces, disclosure, unavailable states, shortcuts and dragging.

It answers the Actions, Whole and parts, and Density and disclosure axes in `REASONING.md`. Floors: `CRAFT.md`. Undo, saving, forms, a command palette, permissions and plans are separate decisions.

## Inputs

- Per action: scope (item, selection, set, page), frequency and source, importance to the job, consequence, status dependence.
- Per task on an item: background kept in view or acted on; length; repetition; own address; width; whether to stop the user.
- Per change: field count, interdependence, one item or many, frequency.
- Occasional versus daily users.
- Each unavailable action's cause: state, prerequisite, permission, plan, failure, never applicable.

## Criteria and evidence

- **Rank and place.** Guidance, untested: place by scope; choose the primary by importance, not frequency alone. Sources disagree on its unit (page, group, region): name it. Guidance separates consequential from benign actions by distance and a second cue.
- **Visibility.** Guidance agrees: critical or frequent actions stay visible; one revealed by hover, gesture or right-click also has a visible route for keyboard and touch. Sources disagree whether a visible menu's actions need another route. Overflow icons hint little at their contents (vendor research); in one experiment, weak signifiers slowed finding visible targets.
- **Surface.** Guidance, untested: modal for short, decisive moments needing nothing behind; nonmodal panel to keep the collection visible; page for long, multi-step or linkable work; in place for frequent small changes. Sources disagree on the default, page or nonmodal: name it.
- **Editing.** The change's shape sets the model; guidance citing mode-error research gives an edit mode several cues. Check where an edited row lands under a sort.
- **Disclosure.** Split on usage data or task analysis; keep what the task needs, and features used together, on one level; name what each trigger reveals. Controlled research shows a trade-off: a reduced start aids learning and findability, a full one awareness of advanced features. Weigh by audience.
- **Unavailable.** Weigh: can this user change it; does the container map every command; must content still be read? Sources disagree on permissions (hide, or disable with a reason), contextual menus (hide or disable), and disabled controls at all (rare, or normal for prerequisites); no controlled study. The reason `CRAFT.md` asks for reaches keyboard and screen-reader users.
- **Shortcuts.** Guidance gives them to core, repeated actions. Several studies agree experienced users seldom adopt them; lab studies raised adoption by showing keys on commands.
- **Dragging.** Sources weigh directness against precision and effort; its role (complementary or essential, main or secondary) sets handle visibility.
- **Standard.** The level `CRAFT.md` targets requires a route without dragging, and single-key shortcuts users can turn off, remap or limit to focus.

## Options space

- **Placement:** header, beside the item, both; one home for all, or not.
- **Visibility:** shown, menu beside the object, revealed.
- **Surface:** page, nonmodal panel, split view, inline expansion, modal.
- **Editing:** per field, row, section or page.
- **Disclosure:** levels; reduced, full, or advanced marked.
- **Unavailable:** hidden, disabled with a reason, focusable but inactive, read-only.
- **Shortcuts:** modifier or single key; discovery by labels, hints after use, tooltips, a list.
- **Dragging:** none, complementary, essential; handle shown, revealed or implied; move menu, arrows, pick-up by click or key.

## Testing

- Findability of hidden actions with newer users; recognition of unused features, besides task time.
- Unprompted drag attempts; shortcut use from logs.
- Each hidden, revealed, unavailable or draggable action by keyboard, touch and screen reader.

## Write down

Each action's place, visibility and frequency source; the primary's unit; surfaces and default side; editing model and mode cues; disclosure split and data; treatment per cause; shortcut and drag routes; menu counts as conventions.
